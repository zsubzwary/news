### Economy
- **JazzCash** and its associated financial services recorded a **67.6%** year-on-year revenue increase to **Rs43.4 billion** in the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. *[Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40447860/jazzcash-records-676pc-increase-in-revenue)*
- **SNGPL** achieved a record profit before levy and income tax of **Rs34,656 million** for FY 2025–26 and recommended a final cash dividend of **Rs3.00 per share (30%)**. *[Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40447847/sngpl-posts-highest-ever-profit-before-levy-income-tax-of-rs34656m)*
- Petrol prices were reduced by **Rs6.59** (now **Rs346/L**) and High-Speed Diesel (HSD) decreased by **Rs4.25** (now **Rs378/L**), effective until November 10. *[Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40447870/petrol-price-cut-by-rs659-hsds-by-rs425)*
- The weekly **Sensitive Price Index (SPI)** inflation rose by **0.29%**, driven by significant price hikes in tomatoes (**54.18%**), LPG (**3.72%**), potatoes (**2.07%**), and eggs (**1.17%**). *[Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40447872/weekly-spi-inflation-up-029pc)*
- **SMEDA** partnered with **ICMA Pakistan** and **DigiKhata** to support the government's goal of increasing SME financing to **Rs2.2 trillion** and expanding the borrower base to **77,000**. *[Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40447869/smeda-partners-with-icma-digikhata)*
- The **Punjab Revenue Authority (PRA)** directed hotel owners to achieve **100% installation** of the **Electronic Invoice Monitoring System (EIMS)** by the end of this month. *[Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40447865/pras-directive-to-hotel-owners)*
- The **FBR** issued **SRO 2063(I)/2026**, mandating sales tax-registered taxpayers to issue advance receipt invoices for all advance payments received. *[Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40447871/fbr-mandates-sales-tax-invoices-for-advance-payments)*
- **RTO Hyderabad** launched an enforcement drive for digital invoicing compliance, inspecting over **30 vehicles** from various business establishments, including cotton factories. *[Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40447867/digital-invoicing-requirements-rto-hyderabad-begins-enforcement-drive)*
- **PPCBL** announced plans to accelerate digital transformation and improve operational efficiency following its 16th Board of Directors meeting. *[Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40447864/ppcbl-set-to-accelerate-digital-transformation)*
- The **Pakistan Agricultural Research Council (PARC)** and the **Central Cotton Research Institute (CCRI)** agreed to enhance scientific cooperation to boost cotton production. *[Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40447863/cotton-production-parc-ccri-agree-to-boost-cooperation)*
- **HBL** launched the **HBL Visa Infinite CreditCard**, a premium offering tailored for affluent and globally connected customers. *[Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40447848/hbl-launches-visa-infinite-creditcard)*
